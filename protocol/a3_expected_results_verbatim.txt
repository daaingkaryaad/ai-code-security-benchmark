T10. Method-Related Expectations
The expected direction was a domain-dependent pattern of potential weakness classes, with no assumed universally safest language. Broad exploratory planning envelopes were 0–60% for any-confirmed-CWE rate and 0–40% for a single CWE frequency in each language-domain cell, with uncommon categories expected to show zeros. Expected instrument problems included false-positive or missed scanner patterns, truncated model responses, parsing failures, and imperfect cross-language dependency equivalence. These broad ranges have no validated statistical confidence meaning. Equal or highly unstable matched outcomes would not justify a language ranking. By condition, the applicable target categories are CWE-89 for database access, CWE-22 for file handling, and CWE-256 and CWE-798 for authentication. These specify the expected weakness classes to examine, without presuming a positive result or a language ranking.
